Local, In-Place and
Sovereign AI 2027 (LISAi-27)

University of Malta
April 15-16, 2027

Artificial intelligence is transforming research and innovation, but one critical question remains: who controls the data, the models, and the infrastructure on which AI depends?

LISAi-27: Local, Inplace and Sovereign AI is a two-day workshop taking place at the University of Malta's Valletta Campus on 15-16 April 2027. The Workshop brings together researchers, technology developers, policymakers and organisations exploring a different way of building and using AI: bringing computation to the data, rather than moving sensitive data to external platforms.

Many of the world's most valuable datasets are not on the open web. They reside in hospitals, biobanks, research institutions, public bodies and businesses, where privacy, governance, security and legal obligations rightly constrain their use. Yet these datasets hold the key to answering important, specialised questions. Advances in open models, efficient machine learning and accessible computing infrastructure are creating opportunities to develop smaller, domain-specific AI systems locally, potentially delivering competitive performance without relying exclusively on large, general-purpose services.

The workshop will explore the technical and organisational foundations of this approach: local and in-place model development, small and midscale AI, AI Commons, privacy-preserving and federated analysis, Trusted Research Environments, data spaces, and the practical meaning of AI sovereignty. Particular attention will be given to biology, medicine and healthcare, where the ability to learn from distributed, sensitive data could accelerate discovery and improve patient care. European initiatives such as EOSC and ELIXIR, together with international standards including those developed by GA4GH, will inform discussions about interoperability and responsible collaboration.

Sovereignty need not mean isolation. A central ambition of LISAi-27 is to understand how institutions can retain meaningful control over data and models while participating in an open, connected research ecosystem.

Designed as a focused gathering of approximately 30-50 participants, LISAi-27 will favour substantive discussion, practical experience and new partnerships over conference scale. Our aim is to help shape an AI future that is not only powerful, but also accessible, collaborative and firmly in the hands of the communities whose data make it possible.
